    Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

    Every trophy is offline and single-player, there are no difficulty trophies and no mission medals, and free roam continues after the story. The order below is built from the official descriptions: story first, then side strands, then the long grinds, then cleanup to 100%.

      Save discipline

      There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 save slots). That is your only way back if you miss the Chapter 1 meditation — the one interaction that can lock the platinum.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is reported as potentially missable. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

      Why only one?

      PowerPyx, KeenGamer and EGamers all flag the same trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Its first meditation — Lucia's on the balcony at Jason's house — is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. No other trophy depends on a one-time moment, because story trophies unlock in order, side strands stay open, and collectibles and activities remain available after the finale.
